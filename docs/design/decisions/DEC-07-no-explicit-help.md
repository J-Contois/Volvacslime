**Summary.** No explicit help: clues live in the world, and every obstacle has several solutions.

## Question
What does "no hints" (pillar 4) forbid and allow?

## Decision
No explicit help, clues in the world. Pillar 4 reads: "Free to experiment: no explicit help, clues in the world, several solutions per obstacle."

| Forbidden | Allowed and wanted |
|---|---|
| Tutorial text, objective markers, help button, highlighting of interactive objects | Frost, burnt cables, labels, the creator's diagrams; rules taught by level design before they are combined |

## Still open
The "Look" reaction (N4) is proposed as allowed only at short range and never pointing at the solution. To confirm.
