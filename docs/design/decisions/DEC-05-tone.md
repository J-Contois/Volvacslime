**Summary.** An unsettling and poetic world with an attaching but not cute creature ("Specimen" direction), PEGI 12.

## Question
GDD open question: cute and poetic, dark and unsettling, or a mix?

## Decision
An unsettling and poetic world, an attaching but not cute creature. A small vulnerable character in a hostile place, without violence or gore, PEGI 12.

## Consequences
- The eyes stay, because they carry the reactions that replace the UI (sheets 3 and 6), but small, without big highlights, and with no mouth or smile. D10 is settled by reviewing the eyes on sheet 1, without a redraw.
- Tone comes from what is cheap for a solo developer: emergency lighting, silence, decay, traces of the incident.
- Kirby stays a mechanics reference, not a tone reference.
