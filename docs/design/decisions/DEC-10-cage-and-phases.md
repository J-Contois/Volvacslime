**Summary.** The cage is the first version of the vertical slice; rooms of the real environment are built after it. Mapping the other GDD phases to studio phases is still open.

## Question
How do the GDD phases map to the studio phases, and what is the scope of the cage?

## Decision
The cage is the FIRST VERSION of the vertical slice; rooms of the real environment come afterwards (user, 2026-11-10).

## Still open
- Mapping of the four undescribed GDD phases to studio phases (producer with the user).
- Where the cage sits in the building (N8).
- Extra slice rooms (a heat or cold source, a digestion-to-fragment case), to scope after the first playtest.

## Proposal (accepted for the first version)
- Only the first GDD phase is detailed; it maps to pre-production, and its gate (one room, one puzzle with two solutions, two outside testers) is the pre-production exit gate.
- The cage alone is enough for that gate.
- For the vertical slice at final quality, the cage is the first room (learn aspirate, spit, throw, break); at least one more room is needed to prove states (DEC-01) and digestion into a fragment (DEC-04).
