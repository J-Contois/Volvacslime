**Summary.** Proposal: aim at the low end of the GDD range (3 to 4 hours) as a ceiling, and cut GDD features in a fixed order when time runs short.

## Question
What is the target duration, and which GDD features are cut first?

## Decision
Not decided.

## Proposal
Ceiling of 3 to 4 hours, reviewed after the vertical slice and after the deadlines are known (Q6). Cut order, first cut at the top, each item weighed against the pillars:

1. Build by assembly as a separate system. Sticking and freezing stay possible as reactions (pillar 2), not as a dedicated feature.
2. Permanent mutations, replaced by the temporary properties of DEC-04.
3. Combat and robots (DEC-03).
4. Multiple endings: from 4 to 1 or 2.
5. Biomes: from 7 to 3 or 4 (for example hall, kitchen, cold room, basement), one state of matter per biome.
6. Split: merged with "spit mass" (N5) rather than cut.

Kept: the verbs, mass, states, data-driven reactions, digestion into fragments.
